## Participant visual recommendation

Use a 4:5 feed master, 1080x1350 px, with a 9:16 Story/Reels crop prepared from the same layout. The participant's own brand should stay first in the hierarchy, while Restok and Brasserie Vivien should appear as equal event partners in a clean lockup.

Recommended hierarchy:
- Top: participant brand name and one-line offer, for example `YOUR BRAND at Restok & Vivien Marché`.
- Middle: one strong product or outfit photo, preferably photographed by the seller, not a collage.
- Bottom: event lockup `Restok x Brasserie Vivien`, date/time, address, and a short CTA.

Example copy overlay:
`YOUR BRAND`
`at Restok & Vivien Marché`
`01.08 | 12:00-17:00`
`Brīvības iela 37`
`Restok x Brasserie Vivien`

Avoid making the creative look like a Brasserie Vivien-only ad. The seller's item and brand should be the focal point; Vivien and Restok provide the event context, location and trust signal.
